WHAT THIS INSTALLS
- Jiv Desktop into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\JivDesktop (just for you, no administrator rights needed).
- A private copy of Python 3.12 (python.org embeddable build, PSF License - see python\LICENSE.txt) with
  FastAPI, Uvicorn, httpx, Pydantic and cryptography (MIT/BSD/Apache licences).
- Your chats, agents and settings are kept in %APPDATA%\JivDesktop. Upgrading keeps them; uninstalling keeps them
  unless you tick "Also delete my data".
- Jiv runs on your computer only (http://127.0.0.1). If Ollama is already installed, its models are found
  automatically; otherwise the Setup Wizard can install it for you.
- This installer is not code-signed. If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click
  "More info" and then "Run anyway".
